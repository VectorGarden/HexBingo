      Rules

        How to play

        Claim a hex
        Click it. Click again to release.
        Block a hex
        Right-click to mark a goal you don't want. Right-click again to clear.
        Track partial progress
        Scroll on a hex, or press + / − while it's focused.
        Fog
        Same board, but only the centre starts visible. Claiming a hex uncovers the ones touching it, so you cannot plan a line in advance — and a goal you block is one you cannot expand through.
        On a phone
        Tap to claim. Press and hold a hex for the actions that have no touch gesture — progress and Block.
        See your lines
        Hover a hex and its three lines light up across the board. Hover a chip in the rail to trace that one line.
        Win
        Complete any of the 15 lines. Short lines are only three hexes but hold the hardest goals — the centre hex is the easiest goal on the board and sits on three full-length lines.
        Pin a line
        Click a chip in the rail to keep it highlighted. Pinned lines get a coloured ring and their hexes a bright border. Click again to unpin, or press Esc to clear.
        Share
        Copy board link puts the seed, game and mode in the URL. Anyone opening it gets the same board.
